HIA_Hou Dataset Background Information
====================================

1. Dataset Overview
-----------------
The HIA_Hou dataset is focused on Human Intestinal Absorption (HIA),
which is a crucial property in drug development and pharmaceutical research.

2. Scientific Background
---------------------
Human Intestinal Absorption (HIA) is the process by which drugs and other
compounds are absorbed from the gastrointestinal tract into the bloodstream.
This property is essential for oral drug delivery and bioavailability.

3. Endpoint Description
--------------------
The endpoint is binary:
- 0: Compound is NOT absorbed through the human intestine
- 1: Compound IS absorbed through the human intestine

4. Practical Significance
----------------------
Understanding HIA is crucial for:
- Drug development and screening
- Optimizing oral drug delivery
- Predicting drug bioavailability
- Reducing costs in drug development
